# M8 — Follow, Track, and Unified News

- **Status:** Not started
- **Repos:** `goldseats-api`, `goldseats-web`
- **Depends on:** [M1](M1.md), [M4](M4.md)
- **Blocks:** [M9](M9.md)

> **Done when:** a logged-in user has a personalised feed and receives a release notification.

---

## Goal

Give people a reason to come back when they're not buying a ticket. Follow films, theatres, and
people; get one feed that aggregates news and release updates from many sources instead of six
newsletters and a subreddit; get told when something you're waiting for opens near you.

This is the retention milestone. [M5](M5.md) makes GoldSeats useful on the night; M8 makes it
useful the rest of the month.

---

## Scope

- `follows` for films, theatres, and people
- `watchlist_entries` — "want to see" and "seen", with optional import
- News aggregation into `news_items` and `news`-type `film_timeline_events`
- `GET /v1/me/feed` — a unified, ranked, per-user feed
- Email notifications for release dates and local showtime availability
- Web push notifications
- Notification preferences and a working unsubscribe
- Authenticated web UI: feed, watchlist, follows management

## Out of scope

- Social features: no reviews, no comments, no ratings, no following other users. Letterboxd
  exists and is good at this. Permanently out of scope per
  [`../roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md#what-were-explicitly-not-building).
- Mobile app push (web push only)
- A digest email. Individual notifications in M8; digests are a later decision driven by
  observed volume.
- Paid news sources
- Recommending *what to watch*. We recommend seats.

## Issue breakdown

### Follows

- [ ] Migration: `follows` with polymorphic `target_type` / `target_id`, per
      [`../../architecture/data-model.md`](../../architecture/data-model.md)
- [ ] `POST /v1/me/follows`, `DELETE /v1/me/follows/{id}`, `GET /v1/me/follows`
- [ ] Duplicate follow returns `409`
- [ ] Per-follow notification toggles: `notify_releases`, `notify_local_showtimes`,
      `notify_news`
- [ ] Scheduled integrity check flagging orphaned `target_id`s — the cost of the polymorphic
      design, paid deliberately
- [ ] Cast and crew ingestion from TMDB so `person` targets are real records, not just IDs
- [ ] `FollowButton` with optimistic update and a proper signed-out prompt
- [ ] Follow counts on film and theatre pages, if they're not embarrassingly low

### Watchlist

- [ ] Migration: `watchlist_entries` — `user_id`, `film_id`, `state`, `seen_at`, `rating`
- [ ] `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /v1/me/watchlist`
- [ ] States: `want_to_see`, `seen`. Distinct from follows — you can follow a film you've seen,
      and watchlist one you don't want news about.
- [ ] Optional rating, for the user's own reference only. **Never aggregated or shown to anyone
      else.**
- [ ] Import: Letterboxd CSV and a plain title list. Fuzzy-match to `films`, show the user what
      matched and what didn't, and let them fix it.
- [ ] Export as CSV. If we hold someone's list, they can take it with them.
- [ ] `/watchlist` route with filters by state

### News aggregation

- [ ] `app/ingest/news.py` — RSS/Atom parsing with `httpx2`
- [ ] **Source allowlist**, each entry reviewed against
      [`../../legal/data-sources-policy.md`](../../legal/data-sources-policy.md): feed URL,
      licence terms, attribution requirement, `robots.txt` check
- [ ] **Respect `robots.txt` and ToS on every source.** Feeds only. No article-body fetching,
      no scraping.
- [ ] Store headline, canonical URL, `source_name`, `author`, `published_at`, and a **short
      summary** — never the full article body
- [ ] Deduplicate on `url` and on `(source, external_id)`
- [ ] Match articles to films by title, with a confidence threshold. Unmatched items are
      industry news with `film_id = NULL`.
- [ ] Create `news`-type `film_timeline_events` for matched items, so [M4](M4.md)'s timeline
      absorbs them without change
- [ ] Hourly schedule, idempotent
- [ ] Per-source failure isolation — one broken feed must not stop the job

### Feed

- [ ] `GET /v1/me/feed` — paginated, from the user's follows
- [ ] Item types: release date announced or changed, trailer, local showtimes available, news,
      festival, award
- [ ] Ranking: recency, `importance`, and follow proximity. Documented, not magic.
- [ ] Deduplicate the same story arriving from several sources
- [ ] `private, no-store`
- [ ] Empty state for a user following nothing, with suggestions — the cold-start problem is the
      main way this feature fails
- [ ] `/feed` route with skeleton loading and infinite or paginated scroll

### Notifications

- [ ] Migration: `notifications` — dispatch log for idempotency. **Notifying twice is worse than
      not notifying.**
- [ ] Email transport with a real provider; configuration per
      [`../../engineering/secrets-and-config.md`](../../engineering/secrets-and-config.md)
- [ ] Templates: release date approaching, release date changed, now playing near you, weekly
      new-showtimes summary
- [ ] Plain-text alternative for every HTML email
- [ ] **One-click unsubscribe**, including the `List-Unsubscribe` header, per follow type and
      globally
- [ ] Web push: VAPID keys, subscription endpoint, `push_subscription_json` on `users`
- [ ] Service worker for push receipt and click-through
- [ ] Permission requested **in context**, after a follow — never on page load
- [ ] Dispatch job every 15 minutes, honouring per-follow preferences
- [ ] Quiet hours: nothing between 10pm and 8am in the user's timezone
- [ ] Rate cap per user per day, so a busy news week can't spam anyone
- [ ] `/settings/notifications` with granular controls and a global off switch
- [ ] Bounce and complaint handling; auto-disable email after repeated hard bounces
- [ ] Delivery logged without message bodies

### Privacy

- [ ] Every new data category added to
      [`../../legal/privacy-policy.md`](../../legal/privacy-policy.md): follows, watchlist,
      seen history, push subscription
- [ ] Account deletion cascades follows, watchlist, notifications, and push subscriptions;
      test proves nothing identifying survives
- [ ] Data export includes follows and watchlist
- [ ] Push subscription deleted on unsubscribe, not just flagged

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications become annoying and users leave permanently | High | Conservative defaults, quiet hours, per-day caps, granular preferences, genuinely easy unsubscribe. Annoying notifications lose users for good. |
| Duplicate notifications | Medium | `notifications` table as an idempotency log, keyed on `(user, follow, event)` |
| News aggregation drifts toward scraping | Medium | Feeds only, reviewed allowlist, headline-plus-summary storage. A PR fetching article bodies gets closed. |
| Copyright exposure from news content | Medium | Headline, link, attribution, and our own short summary. Never full text. |
| Empty feed for new users | Certain | Designed empty state with suggestions; prompt to follow during the [M5](M5.md) flow |
| Feed ranking feels arbitrary | Medium | Document the ranking; keep it simple and explainable rather than clever |
| Email deliverability | Medium | Proper SPF, DKIM, DMARC; bounce handling; a reputable provider |
| Person follows have nothing behind them | High | Cast and crew ingestion is a listed deliverable, not an assumption |
| Watchlist import matches the wrong films | Medium | Show the user what matched, let them correct it, never silently guess |
| Notification job load at scale | Low now | Batch, cap, and monitor. A queue is a later ADR if volume demands it. |

## Done when

**A logged-in user has a personalised feed and receives a release notification.**

Demonstrated by:

1. Signing in, following two films, one theatre, and one person
2. Loading `/feed` and seeing items from all four follows, including at least one aggregated
   news item with visible attribution
3. Adding films to the watchlist, marking one seen, and importing a small list
4. Receiving a real email notification for an upcoming release, with a working one-click
   unsubscribe
5. Granting push permission and receiving a real web push notification
6. Turning off a notification category and confirming it stops
7. Deleting the account and confirming follows, watchlist, and push subscription are gone
